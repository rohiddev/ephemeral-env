  Kubernetes by Spotify — Developer Productivity Benefits

    1. Eliminate context switching
    Developers stay in IDP instead of switching between kubectl, Lens, Rancher, or asking SRE for cluster access. Pod health is on the same page as the service catalog entry.

    2. No cluster access required
    Junior devs and contractors see their service's pod status without needing kubeconfig or namespace RBAC. SRE stops fielding "is my pod running?" questions.

    3. Faster incident response
    During a P1 the developer sees — immediately in IDP — whether their pods are crashing, stuck in pending, or OOMKilled. No war-room delay waiting for SRE to check.

    4. Cross-environment visibility in one view
    Dev, QA, and prod pod counts visible together. Developers know if their last deploy actually rolled out everywhere or only hit one cluster.

    5. Self-service debugging
    Container restart count, last termination reason, resource limit hits — all visible without a support ticket. Developers diagnose their own "why did my pod restart 47 times" before escalating.

    6. Deployment confidence
    After a pipeline run, developer confirms pods are healthy directly in IDP without switching to Harness CD or asking ops. Closes the deploy→verify loop.

    7. Onboarding acceleration
    New team member joins, opens the service in IDP, sees exactly which clusters and namespaces their service runs in — no documentation hunting.
