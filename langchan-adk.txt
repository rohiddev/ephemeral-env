LangGraph feels like a graph DSL — powerful, but you're working at a lower abstraction level.
It rewards engineers who want transparency and deterministic behavior.
The cost: more boilerplate, steeper learning curve, fragmented documentation. GitHub

LangGraph gives you an explicit graph (state machine) to control agent flows with precision,
