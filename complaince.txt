You built a UI with proper auth/authz and role-based access for OpenShift cluster actions

When triggered that way, there's no attribution (you can't tell who triggered it) and Jenkins' own history rolls off after a few days
This creates an audit/compliance gap: actions are happening against OpenShift with no durable, attributable record
